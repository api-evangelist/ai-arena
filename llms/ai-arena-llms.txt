# AI Arena (ArenaX Labs)

> ArenaX Labs is the company behind AI Arena, an Ethereum/Arbitrum-native web3 fighting game where players purchase, train (via imitation learning), and battle characters powered by real AI, with a native $NRN token. The company also builds developer infrastructure for reinforcement learning: RLMesh, an open-source, Gymnasium-compatible framework that connects RL models to environments across process, dependency, and machine boundaries over gRPC; and SAI (competesai.com), a gamified RL research and competition platform.
